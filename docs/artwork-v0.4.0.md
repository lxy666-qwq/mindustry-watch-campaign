# v0.4.0 连续地图美术

两张地图使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，作为战役导航的连续美术底图。每张原始PNG为2172×724；未进行图像裁剪、拼接或二次缩放写回。游戏按原始宽高比例显示。

- Serpulo：`core/assets/route-maps/serpulo-v0.4.0.png`。
- Erekir：`core/assets/route-maps/erekir-v0.4.0.png`。

主题依据56张任务初始地图的原生地形提取记录 `core/assets/route-scenes/index.tsv`，例如零号/冰冻森林的雪地，群岛/海岸的深水与沙地，孢子区的苔藓，Erekir的黄色岩层、熔渣与晶石。美术中的山脉、建筑和道路是连续场景的示意绘制，并非真实扇区坐标或关卡建筑的逐格还原。关卡详情另保留对应实际初始地图缩影，以便对照。绘制道路不决定解锁；悬停标记高亮的联系来自真实关卡前置。

以下为实际内置工具提示词；未引用第三方图片或游戏美术素材。

```text

It must be one seamless, continuous panoramic landscape, NOT separate thumbnails, cards, biome bubbles, or a node diagram. Original art with the hand-painted, storybook overhead/isometric campaign-map feeling of Kingdom Rush, but original Mindustry-inspired science-fiction forts and mining outposts, no copied game assets. The whole landscape is connected naturally through mountains, rivers, coastline, bridges, winding earth roads, and rail/mining trails. Clearly modeled rocky relief, forests, waterfalls, lakes, little ruins, watchtowers, small settlements, mechanical fortresses, ships, docks, mines, pipes, ruined factories, tiny resource crates, varied vegetation, cliff shadows, little scenic discoveries. Cohesive ink contours and textured gouache coloring, warm inviting handcrafted illustration, readable silhouettes, highly polished game art. No huge blank spaces. No text, numbers, star icons, locks, labels, UI, borders, circles, badges, flags, or stage buttons; the game will overlay interactive markers.
This is the Serpulo world, artwork inspired by actual mission biomes. Left to right, transition through six connected regions (approximate sixths of the width), with smaller varied pockets flowing into neighbors: 1 snow-covered starting valley, frozen pine forest, grey basalt cliffs, with a green fortified meadow side valley and a dark twin canyon; 2 snow craters, purple-spore moss woodland and abandoned biomass factories, a frozen lake citadel; 3 ruinous sandy coast, deep-blue sea and windswept islands linked by wooden docks/bridges, flooding river valley, harbor and slate mining mountains; 4 fungal mountain pass, infected dark canyons, atolls, submerged piers and thick purple-green mycelial forests with a mossy bastion; 5 ochre sand dunes, black tar pools, red-sand caravan outpost, crashed industrial vessel embedded in a snowy high plateau, nuclear ruins; 6 desolate hot-rock rift with smoke, a large navy fortress and coastal shipyard, and a technological planetary launch citadel at the far right coast. Fill the full canvas as a beautifully connected illustrated world, no isolated circular terrain patches, no sparse flat background. ## Erekir
```

```text
Create a polished, densely illustrated original 2D campaign-world panorama for the Mindustry-inspired Erekir planet. One continuous richly painted landscape, requested 3840 x 1280 (3:1), edge-to-edge physical terrain, natural transitions, suitable for a game camera to pan across. Original handcrafted storybook overhead/isometric tower-defense campaign-map art, with the visual richness and clear relief of classic Kingdom Rush campaign maps, but entirely original science-fiction locations and constructions. Match a warm illustrated ink-and-gouache world-map aesthetic, no photorealism. Absolutely NO separate vignette cards, isolated terrain circles, diagram lines, empty flat backgrounds, UI, text, letters, numbers, labels, pins, badges, stars or flags. The game will add real interactive markers.
Fill every region with cliffs, paths, little mechanical forts, mining bases, furnaces, industrial ruins, bridges, canyon stairways, pipes, towers, lavafalls, dense rock strata and other small discoveries. Roads are painted physical winding tracks across valleys and cliffs, connected with bridges and stairs, with scenic side paths. This is a hot alien world with ochre yellow-stone plateaus, rhyolite red cliffs, dark carbon basins, turquoise beryl and violet crystalline mountains; no snow. Across the panorama left to right: 1 a gold-red stone starting valley with a small fortified mechanical outpost and an ochre shield bastion, and a large brilliant orange molten-slag lake slightly further right; 2 branching sunken canyon crossroads, a huge atlas plateau with a storm-shrouded dark side highland, a dramatically split rock ridge and terraced basin with mining machinery; 3 turquoise and petrol-green alien marsh/arkycite pools threaded with channels, a purple-spore forest side valley and power outpost, yellow mountain peaks and red ravine; 4 a massive volcanic caldera with lavafalls and a nearby lava fault side fortress, gradually transitioning to shining teal/purple crystalline-stone stronghold, narrow crystal crevice and siege ramparts; 5 rusty converging badland roads and iron-stone karst labyrinths leading to a black-carbon end fortress, with a separate final side citadel and fiery industrial defenses. Unify the full image using winding terrain, a mountain range near the upper edge, multiple small rivers of lava and continuous rock relief. Put playable scenic clearings at various heights through the middle 70%, keep detailed foreground cliffs and distant mountains. Substantial variation and hand-drawn detail everywhere, coherent world, no large blank patches. Original game art, no logos, no copied assets.
```
